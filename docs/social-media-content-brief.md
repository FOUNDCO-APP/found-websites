## Found positioning

Found is not being positioned as a website builder like Wix or Squarespace.

Use this framing:

- Found was built for business owners who are already doing the real work.
- Found helps a business stay easy to find, easy to trust, and easy to choose.
- Found helps turn attention into action: calls, messages, orders, estimates, bookings, leads, and customer next steps.
- Found helps owners keep the business current from their phone.
- Found should feel like relief, not more software.

Avoid this framing:

- "Website builder" as the main identity.
- "Build your website" as the core promise.
- Feature-list-only copy such as "photos, hours, services" unless tied to a bigger outcome.
- Corporate, fluffy, AI-sounding captions.
- "Found is being built" or "why we are building Found." Use "Found was built" when describing the product's origin.

## Approved creative principles

1. One idea per post.
2. Make the image understandable in 5 seconds.
3. The image should not always look the same.
4. Not every post needs the Found logo.
5. Do not put the phone/logo motif on everything.
6. Avoid repeating the same dark Found overlay formula too often.
7. Mix formats:
   - full realistic business-owner/customer photo with little or no image text;
   - solid color typography post;
   - Nextdoor-style bold promise post;
   - founder note / personal note;
   - product education post;
   - emotional small-business reality post.
8. Real beats posed. The business owner should usually be doing something, not staring into camera.
9. Use captions to carry the deeper message. Image copy should stay simple.
10. Strip image metadata before final delivery. Final PNG metadata should verify as `{}`.

Brand colors used:

- Found black: near `#080A09`.
- Found green: near `#32D074`.
- Off-white paper: near `#F4F1E8`.

Typography:

- Use clean system typography when making deterministic social assets.
- Recent scripts use Windows Segoe UI files:
  - `C:\Windows\Fonts\segoeui.ttf`
  - `C:\Windows\Fonts\segoeuib.ttf`
- Watch line wrapping carefully. Do not leave awkward one-word breaks like `should / be` unless it is intentional and visually balanced.

Image generation rules:

- No readable fake brand names.
- No watermarks.
- No fake app UI unless intentionally blurred/unreadable.
- No distorted hands, extra fingers, or AI-looking faces.
- Avoid glossy stock-photo perfection.
- Prefer natural work moments.

## Caption voice

Captions must feel like a real person wrote them.

Use:

- direct language;
- short lines;
- plain examples;
- emotional truth from the owner's day;
- a simple CTA.

Avoid:

- fake enthusiasm;
- over-polished marketing language;
- long abstract claims;
- robotic frameworks;
- too much cleverness before people know Found.

Facebook captions may include the actual URL:

```text
Start here: https://foundco.app
```

Instagram captions should usually say:

```text
Start here: link in bio.
```

Reason: Instagram feed captions usually do not make normal links clickable, so the full URL can feel clunky.

## Growth strategy (team meeting, September 7, 2026)

Held with Steve (message), Phil (growth), Jony (design), Angela (customer emotion). Shawn endorsed the framework and asked it be saved as the working direction. Five tactical decisions are still open (see "Open concerns and pending decisions").

### The situation
The account has ~27 posts up and near-zero reach (view counts of 1-7). Not a content-quality problem. Two real problems: the account is cold, and the posts have leaned too abstract ("Your business has a feeling").

### Steve's filter (applies to every post)
Every post must say one specific, true, concrete thing. If we cannot name what the viewer should DO after seeing it, it does not go up. No more mood statements.

### Phil — how reach actually works in 2026 (the honest version)
1. **Static images alone will not grow a cold account.** Reach on IG and FB now rewards watch time, saves, shares, sends -> that means **Reels and carousels**, not single images. This is the single biggest lever. Every week's plan carries Reels + carousels for this reason.
2. **Hook or die.** First line of every caption and first frame of every image/video must stop the scroll: a question, a number, or a sharp claim. No warm-ups.
3. **One CTA per post, matched to platform.** Facebook posts carry the `https://foundco.app` URL and drive clicks. Instagram feed barely passes link clicks -> IG's job is saves, follows, profile visits; link-in-bio catches the ready ones. Do not expect IG feed to send traffic; expect it to build the audience that later clicks.
4. **Stories every day.** 1-3 low-effort frames (reshare the feed post with a personal line, a poll, behind-the-scenes). Keeps the account active and drives profile visits.
5. **Engage out, 15 min/day.** Comment as Found on small-business-owner and niche-creator posts. Cold accounts grow in other people's comment sections.
6. **Consistency beats volume.** 3/day every day beats 7 then 0. Lock the three windows (8:30-9:30 AM, 12-1:30 PM, 5-6:30 PM Arizona).
7. **After 7 days of real data**, put `$5-10/day` behind the two best performers for a week. Cheap, and it warms the retargeting pool for real ads later.

### Jony — design container system (rotate so the grid breathes)
The grid looks same-y because too many posts use one container: dark photo, centered white text, green word. Rotate seven:
- Full-bleed photo, no text or tiny corner text
- Solid Found-green typography card
- Solid Found-black typography card
- Off-white paper card (founder notes, "truths")
- Photo with a clean lower-third text band
- Carousel template: off-white, green accent bar, page dots, consistent
- Video: black frame, green captions, FOUND mark only on the end card

Rules: no more than one dark-photo-plus-centered-text post per day. FOUND wordmark is not on everything. Green is an accent, not always a background.

### Angela — content ratio for weeks 1-2
The audience does not know Found. Lead with "this brand understands my day," not "buy this."
- ~60% relatable / useful
- ~30% product-in-context
- ~10% direct ask / founder
Every post: make them feel seen first, show one concrete relief second. Do not open a week with the hard CTA.

### Post-type rotation (7 types)
1. **Owner-reality photo** - real owner mid-work, minimal text, caption carries it.
2. **Bold promise** - Nextdoor-style, solid color, one line.
3. **Carousel** - how-to / checklist, built for the save.
4. **Product-in-context** - one specific thing Found does (edit hours, a lead arriving, an estimate sent). Video where possible.
5. **Founder note** - Shawn's first-person voice, paper card.
6. **Reel / short video** - text-on-motion, screen-recording, or owner b-roll + captions. Highest reach potential.
7. **Customer-POV moment** - relatable "you're the customer deciding" scene.

## Scheduling tool - DECIDED

**Tool: Metricool.** Chosen September 7, 2026 after comparing Metricool, Publer, Buffer, and Meta Business Suite against live pricing.

Account state:
- On the Metricool **free plan** now. Facebook + Instagram connected.
- Facebook currently mirrors Instagram (same posts) until there is momentum to justify per-platform variants.
- Free plan cap: **20 posts/month**. At 3+/day this lasts about a week.
- Upgrade path: **Starter, annual billing (~`$20/month`, ~`$240/year`)** the moment the 20-post cap bites. Starter adds unlimited publishing, analytics with unlimited history, competitor tracking, PDF/PPT reports, CSV bulk upload, and scheduled recycling - all under one "brand," covering every connected network.

Why Metricool over the others:
- **Per-brand pricing.** One flat price covers Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Google Business Profile together. Publer charges per social account (~`$5` base + `$4` each on Professional), and its analytics + recycling are locked to the ~`$42/month` Business tier. Buffer is per-channel and its YouTube support is Shorts-only.
- Adding TikTok, YouTube, and Google Business Profile later costs nothing extra on Metricool.
- Analytics is "nice, not necessary" per Shawn - Metricool includes it at base, not as a paid add-on.
- Meta Business Suite is free but Facebook/Instagram only; it cannot carry the plan to add TikTok/YouTube/GBP.

Do NOT build a custom auto-posting system yet. Build the content production workflow first. A future custom system could still store: image/video asset, per-platform caption, hashtags, story text, publish date/time, posted status, performance notes. Actual auto-posting through APIs adds permissions/app-review complexity, especially for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

### Measure at 24 hours, every post
reach; profile visits; likes; saves; comments; website clicks; follows. After two weeks, let Found's own audience data beat this plan.
