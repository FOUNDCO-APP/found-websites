## Goal for Week 1

Cold account, near-zero reach. Week 1 is not about selling — it is about proving to a stranger that Found understands their day, and getting the first saves, shares, profile visits, and clicks. Move people to **view, save, follow, and click through to foundco.app**.

## Rules carried from the brief

- 3 posts/day at 8:30–9:30 AM, 12–1:30 PM, 5–6:30 PM Arizona.
- One idea per post. Say one specific, true, concrete thing. If you can't name what the viewer should DO, don't post it.
- Rotate the 7 design containers. Max one dark-photo-plus-centered-text post per day.
- Hook in the first caption line and first image/video frame.
- One CTA per post. Facebook = the `https://foundco.app` URL. Instagram = "Start here: link in bio."
- IG hashtags: 5 max, rotated (`#FoundCo` + industry + owner + problem/benefit + discovery). Facebook: 1–2.
- Story every day (1–3 low-effort frames).
- Strip image metadata; final PNG metadata must verify as `{}`. Feed size `1080x1350`. Reels/Stories `1080x1920`.
- Content ratio for the week: ~60% relatable/useful, ~30% product-in-context, ~10% direct ask/founder.

## Design system quick-reference (full detail in the brief)

- Colors: Found black `#080A09`, Found green `#32D074`, off-white paper `#F4F1E8`.
- Typography: clean system type (Segoe UI files listed in the brief). Watch line wrapping — no awkward one-word breaks.
- 7 containers, rotated: full-bleed photo · solid green card · solid black card · off-white paper card · photo + lower-third band · carousel template (off-white, green accent bar, page dots) · video (black frame, green captions, FOUND mark on end card only).
- FOUND wordmark is NOT on every post. Green is an accent, not always a background.
- Image rules: no readable fake brand names, no watermarks, no fake app UI unless intentionally blurred, no distorted hands/AI faces, avoid glossy stock perfection, prefer real work moments.

## Production workflow (approved September 8, 2026)

1. Codex produces in batches (e.g. 6-7 at a time): image/video concept -> final asset -> FB caption -> IG caption (5 hashtags) -> Story line.
3. Save FB + IG versions in `social-assets/facebook/` and `social-assets/instagram/` with a clear slug (`found-<platform>-week01-day1-am-<slug>-1080x1350.png`).
4. Strip metadata, verify size and `{}`.
5. Shawn redlines the batch.
6. Approved posts go into Metricool at the correct day/time.
7. Log each one in `docs/social-post-log.md` (posting record table).
8. Paid ads are allowed. Team default: after 7 days of data, boost the best two posts at a small test budget before scaling.

5. **Production model — approved.** Codex drafts batches, Shawn redlines, approved posts are scheduled into Metricool and logged.
